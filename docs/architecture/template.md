# NNNN — Title

- **Status:** Proposed
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD

## Context

What forced a decision: the constraints that were real at the time, not the ones that look
important in hindsight.

## Decision

What was decided, in a sentence or two, followed by the detail needed to act on it. Name the types
and members that carry the decision, so the record can be checked against the code.

## Consequences

What follows from it — including what it costs and what it makes harder. A record whose
consequences are all benefits has not been written honestly.

## Alternatives considered

What else was on the table, and why it was not chosen. This is the section worth the most in two
years, and the one that is lost entirely if the record is never written.
